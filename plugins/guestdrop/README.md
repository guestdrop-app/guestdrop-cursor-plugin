# GuestDrop for Cursor

Connects Cursor to the GuestDrop MCP server at `https://guestdrop.app/api/mcp`.

GuestDrop lets people who host weddings, parties and other events collect guests' photos and short videos through a QR code. Guests scan and upload with no app and no account. With this plugin, the event's creator can:

- create an event and get the guest upload link, a QR code image and the gallery link
- list events and check upload counts, privacy and when the gallery closes
- page through uploads, hide items from the guest gallery, and delete photos or videos after confirming
- check the Google Drive backup and retry failed backups
- get a checkout link to keep the gallery open longer (payment happens in the browser)

Sign-in is OAuth with the creator's GuestDrop account. Setup guide and privacy details: https://guestdrop.app/mcp
